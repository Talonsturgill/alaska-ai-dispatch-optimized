48 THE SHAPE OF THIS ONE IS THE LESSON. A hollow card asserts nothing and is merely weak. Adding a data column to it made it wrong, and it did so on the thesis card of a film whose entire argument is the gap between what a statute says and what people assume it says. The judge who found it also flagged its provenance unprompted, so the panel would read their two-point accuracy drop as the fix's cost rather than as a moved goalpost. 49 render_parallel.sh fingerprints the engine source before the first chunk and after the last. Each chunk runs its own remotion render and bundles from source when THAT CHUNK starts, so an edit landing mid render is picked up by the chunks that have not begun and missed by the ones already running, and the concatenated result is part old film and part new with no seam anyone can detect. The freshness check downstream structurally cannot catch this, because it compares the finished mp4's mtime against the newest source and a mixed render is NEWER than every source: it looks fresh precisely because it is. This is a warning rather than a hard failure because a comment only edit changes the fingerprint without changing a pixel, which is exactly what happened the day it was written.
50 AND THE MISTAKE THAT PROMPTED IT IS THE MORE USEFUL HALF. A fixer edited the episode two minutes after a render had been launched, so the render was killed on the mtime alone to avoid shipping a mixed film. The edit turned out to be a comment. The visual work was already in the commit taken minutes earlier, and about five minutes of render was thrown away for nothing. An mtime says THAT a file changed, never WHETHER the change reaches a frame, and the diff was one command away. Check what changed before acting on the fact that something did.
51 A related habit worth dropping: committing another agent's file while it is still editing.
