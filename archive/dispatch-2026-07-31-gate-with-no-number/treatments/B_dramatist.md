# B. THE DRAMATIST — "The Gate With No Number"
LOGLINE: A gate comes down across Alaska's data centers and the dial that decides who it catches has nothing written on it, so the facility it stops hardest is the one that makes its own power at the wellhead.
THE OPPOSING FORCE IS A BLANK DIAL. Nobody lies, hides or profiteers. Every decent person in frame is standing where the line should be.
COLD OPEN: boom SLAMS at 1.3s, camera punches in on the road scale plate, needle whips and rattles against the pin, and the dial has NO GRADATIONS. VO "A gate just came down on Alaska's data centers. Nobody wrote the number on it."
STRUCTURE: Act1 the money deflated fast then the three existing gates. Act2 north to state land, 715.4 acres, 50 years, the pen that never signs, 500 comments, then the film concedes its own weakness on camera (patchwork). Act3 TWIN GATES side by side.
THE TURN: two identical rigs. NEW YORK has real gradations with a band at HYPERSCALE, a clock WITH HANDS, a one-year bezel, a GEIS bell. ALASKA is the same rig, blank plate, clock with NO HANDS. Nobody says "broader". The camera holds 0.9s and lets two objects be different.
OPEN LOOP: FatArrow taps the blank dial twice at 17.6s, "two numbers decide who this catches, neither is in that sentence". Paid 60.6s at the twin gates.
BUTTON: the two missing numbers ARE the two questions. WHAT SIZE TRIGGERS IT. WHAT ENDS IT. Dial still blank at the end because a film about an unanswered question should not pretend to close it.
NET-NEW: ThresholdGate. marks 0..1 (size), hands 0..1 (time), lamp only on a real decision.
SIGNATURE: the wellhead machine with its own generator, boom resting on its roof, and a dashed line from a lit Anchorage kitchen stretching 250 miles and STOPPING DEAD in open tundra. The gap is the widest space in frame.
SELF-FLAG: Act1 spends 11s on money the thesis does not need. Whole film rests on a net-new hero reading at frame size (3 prior shelf failures of exactly that kind).
