# The Field That Stopped in 2019

THE FIELD THAT STOPPED IN 2019. Alaska widened a routine voter-roll question by orders of magnitude without widening anything that catches its mistakes, so about 3,500 citizens got a letter questioning their citizenship because a DMV field still says what was true the day they got their license. Nobody lost the vote. The asymmetry is the lesson, and no AI was involved at all, which is exactly why it transfers.

## Angle
Alaska widened a routine records check by orders of magnitude without widening anything that catches its mistakes, so about 3,500 citizens got a letter questioning their citizenship because a DMV field still says what was true the day they got their license. Nobody lost the vote. The asymmetry is the lesson.

## Turn
All four analysts independently converged on the same image, which is why it is the turn. One person, two records, six years apart. A DMV card stamped 2019 with its citizenship field greying over, and a naturalization certificate stamped 2025, clean and bright. An arrow leaves the 2025 certificate, travels toward the DMV card, and stops dead at a blank wall between them. Neither record is wrong. The update simply never had a road to travel.

## Signature shot
The funnel drawn wrong. Its mouth swings open from a trickle of names to a flood of records in one motion, while the stem underneath never changes width. It stays one phone line, one mailbox, one clerk. The overflow spills over the sides as envelopes.
