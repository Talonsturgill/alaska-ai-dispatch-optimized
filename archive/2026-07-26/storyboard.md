# The Field That Stopped in 2019

THE FIELD THAT STOPPED IN 2019. Alaska widened a routine voter-roll question by orders of magnitude without widening anything that catches its mistakes, so about 3,500 citizens got a letter questioning their citizenship because a DMV field still says what was true the day they got their license. Nobody lost the vote. The asymmetry is the lesson, and no AI was involved at all, which is exactly why it transfers.
