A SECOND UNREPORTED SCOOP IS SITTING THERE. HHS finalized killing the Alaska-only SEDS-AK grant line on July 28 (91 FR 47241) and stood up a 3.5 million dollar national "Artificial Intelligence for American Indians" institute instead. The federal document itself records that commenters warned the change would disadvantage Alaska Tribes, and the agency finalized it anyway. No Alaska outlet has it. It lost to today's story only because it is a grant-design story rather than a working system, and because its natural staging is a characterized paperwork machine, which is what the 07-26 dispatch already was.

It came from a researcher's paraphrase and I promoted it to a quote. It also killed my original Alaska hook, because the study area's nearest point to the coast is 204 km, not "directly off the North Slope," and it ruled that any claim nobody was consulted is unverifiable and probably false, since the statutorily required subsistence determination sits in nine pages of the notice that could not be retrieved. Nothing in the film makes that claim.
